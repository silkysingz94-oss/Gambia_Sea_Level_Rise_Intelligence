METHODOLOGY & DATA SOURCES

Administrative boundaries: Census_EAs_Geo.json hosted in the SILKYSINGZ GitHub repository.

Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles (Terrarium encoding). Elevation is decoded from RGB tile values.

Scenario calculation: Annual sea-level rise rate × years after 2026, converted from millimetres to metres. Tide / surge allowance and vertical datum adjustment are added separately.

Screening method: Terrain samples at or below the selected threshold are classified as potentially exposed. This is a simplified elevation-threshold screening model, not a hydrodynamic flood simulation.

The sampled area is an approximate grid-cell estimate, not a surveyed inundation boundary. Results depend on DEM resolution, tile availability, vertical datum and selected scenario assumptions.

This model does not simulate coastal connectivity, river flow, drainage, tides, erosion, sea defences, subsidence or wave dynamics. It must not be interpreted as an official flood forecast or population exposure estimate.
