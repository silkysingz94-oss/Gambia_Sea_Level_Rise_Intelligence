Methodology & limitations

Administrative filtering: RCO, lganame, distname, wardname and eaname filter the supplied EA records. feacode is retained as the EA identifier. Area_sqkm is used for EA area totals.

Population: The population selector uses a numeric attribute present in the uploaded JSON. The dashboard does not generate missing population values.

Elevation: Terrarium RGB tiles are decoded to elevation values. A regular grid is sampled inside selected EA polygons.

Screening: Cells at or below the user-defined threshold are highlighted. EA centroid elevations are used for the EA screening indicator.

This is a simplified bathtub-style elevation screening, not a hydrodynamic model. It does not simulate tides, wave propagation, storm surge dynamics, river discharge, drainage, barriers, erosion, subsidence or hydraulic connectivity to the sea. Vertical datum compatibility must be verified before interpreting the absolute elevation threshold.
