DATA & SCIENTIFIC METHOD
Administrative boundaries: Census_EAs_Geo.json from GitHub.
Administrative attributes: RCO, lganame, distname, wardname, feacode, eaname, Area_sqkm.
Elevation: AWS Terrain Tiles, Terrarium encoding.
Simulation: DEM elevation threshold against a user-defined water-level scenario.
Scenario equation: rate × years after 2026 + baseline offset + tide/surge allowance.

This is a bathtub-style elevation screening model. It does not simulate tides, waves, coastal barriers, drainage, erosion, hydrodynamic connectivity or saltwater intrusion.
